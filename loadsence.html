What this is, honestly: a software simulation, as the challenge allows. Waveforms are generated from typical induction-motor behaviour (single-phase, 50 Hz), and the thresholds are hand-set rules chosen so a plant electrician can follow them. Real deployment needs per-phase sensing and calibration on real machines, which is what the pilot stage is for.
